Will You Be My Valentine? 💌

For Nodi 💖

My Dearest Nodi,

I don’t really know the exact moment when you became so important to me. Maybe it was in the small conversations we shared, or maybe it was the way you smile without even trying. But somewhere along the way, you slowly became the most beautiful part of my ordinary days.

There’s something about you that feels different — calm, genuine, and real. When life feels heavy, just thinking about you makes it lighter. When my mind is full of noise, you are the quiet comfort that makes everything feel okay again.

I admire the way you are — the way you talk, the way you think, the way you care. You may not realize it, but you have this rare ability to make someone feel seen and understood without saying much.

I won’t promise you a perfect fairytale, because real feelings are better than perfect stories. But I promise honesty. I promise respect. I promise effort — every single day. I promise to stand beside you, not just in happy moments, but in the difficult ones too.

I want to support your dreams, listen to your worries, celebrate your victories, and create memories that we’ll smile about years from now. I want to be someone you can trust, someone you can lean on, someone who truly values you.

Nodi, you are not just someone I like — you are someone I deeply care about.

So today, with a hopeful heart and a sincere smile, I want to ask you something simple yet meaningful —

Will you be my Valentine? 💕

No matter what your answer is, I’m grateful that you are in my life. You already make it brighter just by being you.

With all my heart,
Pritam 💌
