Will You Be My Valentine? 💌

For Nodi 💖

My Dearest Nodi,

From the very first moment you came into my life, something quietly changed inside me. Ordinary days started feeling special. Your smile, your presence, even the smallest conversations with you stay in my heart longer than you know.

You have this beautiful way of making everything feel lighter. When life gets heavy, just thinking about you makes it softer. Being around you feels peaceful, safe, and real.

I don’t promise perfection. But I promise honesty, effort, respect, and care. I promise to stand beside you, support your dreams, and celebrate every small victory with you.

I want to be the reason you smile on difficult days. I want to create memories with you that we’ll laugh about years later. You’re not just someone I like — you’re someone I truly care about.

So here I am, asking from my heart — Will you be my Valentine? 💕

With all my heart,
Pritam 💌
